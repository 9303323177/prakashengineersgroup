Our Services

From crane manufacturing to ongoing maintenance — we cover the complete lifecycle of industrial crane solutions.

Crane Manufacturing

We manufacture industrial cranes to customer specifications — capacity, span, lifting height, and application are all configured as per your requirement.

Crane Installation

We handle complete crane installation at the customer's site — including girder assembly, runway setup, mechanical and electrical connections.

Commissioning Support

After installation, we carry out commissioning — testing crane operation, checking limit switches, load testing, and ensuring everything operates safely.

Crane Service

Preventive Maintenance

Breakdown & Repair Support

When a crane breaks down, we respond promptly. Our team diagnoses the issue and carries out the necessary repair to restore normal operation.

Inspection & Technical Support

After-Sales Support

Crane Maintenance

Why Crane Maintenance Matters

In industrial operations, a crane is the heart of material movement. Scheduled maintenance is essential for safe, reliable, and uninterrupted production.

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled checks and servicing prevent small issues from becoming major breakdowns. We follow a systematic approach to keep your crane in good working condition.

Inspection & Checking

Regular inspection of mechanical and electrical components identifies wear and potential failures before they affect crane operation or safety.

Breakdown Support

When a breakdown occurs, rapid response is critical. We provide timely breakdown support to minimize production downtime and restore operation.

Component Condition Checking

We check the condition of all key components — mechanical, electrical, and structural — and recommend replacements when required to maintain safe operation.

Operational Reliability

A well-maintained crane is a reliable crane. Regular maintenance reduces unexpected downtime and supports consistent production output in your facility.

Long-Term Equipment Support

We provide long-term maintenance support and after-sales assistance. Our goal is to help you get the maximum productive life from your crane investment.
